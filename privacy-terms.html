Privacy Policy & Terms and Conditions

Last Updated: 2026

1) About Our Service

The Floral Hugs is an online booking and facilitation platform that enables customers to place booking requests for cakes, flower bouquets, and related gift items with home delivery services. We do not manufacture, produce, or store cakes, flowers, or any listed products ourselves.

All orders are fulfilled through independent and trusted local shopkeepers, vendors, and partners. Product availability, preparation, and delivery are subject to vendor confirmation and operational feasibility.

2) Booking Request vs Order Confirmation

Submitting a booking form on our website only represents a booking request and does not guarantee order confirmation.

  • An order is considered confirmed only after our team contacts the customer via WhatsApp, phone call, or message.
  • Confirmation is provided after verification of order details, delivery location, availability, and other necessary requirements.

Until such confirmation is communicated by The Floral Hugs, customers should not assume that the booking has been finalized.

3) Payment Policy

  • Advance payment is mandatory for order confirmation and processing.
  • Orders will not be confirmed, prepared, or delivered without successful prepaid payment.
  • Payment indicates the customer’s consent and intent to proceed with the requested booking.

4) Cancellation and Refund Policy

  • If an order is cancelled within 20 minutes of successful payment, the customer is eligible for a 100% refund.
  • In cases where cancellation occurs after order confirmation or at the time of delivery, the following charges will apply:
    • Cake orders: 70% of the total order amount will be charged.
    • Flower bouquet orders: 50% of the total order amount will be charged.
  • Refunds, where applicable, will be processed to the original payment method within a reasonable processing period.

5) Delivery Accessibility Policy

Delivery services are provided strictly to locations that are accessible by a motorable road and can be reached using a standard delivery vehicle.

In situations where the delivery address is not reachable by a motorable road and requires the delivery personnel to travel on foot, via trails, stairs, narrow pathways, or any non-vehicular access routes, delivery services will not be feasible.

The customer is solely responsible for ensuring that the provided delivery address is fully accessible by a standard delivery vehicle at the time of delivery.

If the delivery location is found to be non-motorable, partially inaccessible, restricted, unsafe, or not legally permitted for vehicle entry, The Floral Hugs reserves the right to cancel the order, reschedule the delivery, or request an alternate delivery location without any liability.

No delivery attempts will be made to areas where vehicle access is restricted, unsafe, or prohibited due to terrain, regulations, or safety concerns.

6) Customer Information and Self-Declaration

By submitting a booking request, the customer confirms that all provided information, including name, contact number, delivery address/location, and order details, is accurate, complete, and truthful.

The Floral Hugs reserves the right to cancel any booking request or confirmed order if incorrect, false, or misleading information is identified at any stage of processing.

7) Privacy Policy

We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. The information collected is limited strictly to what is necessary for order processing.

  • We may collect details such as name, phone number, delivery location/address, order details, and event date.
  • This information is used solely for booking confirmation, customer communication, delivery coordination, and service improvement.
  • We do not sell, rent, or misuse customer data under any circumstances.
  • Customer information may be shared only with delivery partners or vendors when required to fulfill the order.

8) Contact, Service Limitations, and Policy Updates

Delivery timelines and service availability may be affected by factors such as vendor availability, traffic conditions, weather, or other unforeseen circumstances beyond our control. While we strive to ensure timely and reliable service, delays caused by third-party vendors or external conditions cannot always be avoided.

The Floral Hugs reserves the right to update, modify, or revise these Terms and Privacy Policy at any time without prior notice. Continued use of the website or services indicates acceptance of the updated policies.
